# Five-minute executive demo

1. Run `python3 server.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, and click **Demo mode**.
2. On **Fan request**, click Front Floor and Front Lower Bowl on the signature stadium map. Keep quantity 2, open **Improve my chances**, and enable fewer tickets, alternate performance, and VIP. Click **Review request**; point out the no-timing-advantage promise and editable receipt.
3. Open **Control room**. Read the 500K fans, ~1.25M tickets, 68.5K available supply, and demand/supply ratio. Scan zone pressure and overlapping cohort context.
4. Click **Preview** beside production holds. Explain the before/after estimate, then preview a **second performance**. Move a price slider and emphasize bounds and common product prices.
5. Click **Run deterministic clearing**. Explain that eligibility, inventory, adjacency, cohort policy, and a seeded lottery—not arrival—determine results.
6. Open **Negotiation**. Read Maya's near-miss conversation. Emphasize that every alternative has an approved catalog price and acceptance changes the feasible request; AI neither allocates nor prices.
7. Open **Compare**. Show the timing permutation proof, peak-load bars, fan active time, sell-through, intervention window, and bot-speed comparison.
8. Close with: “Instead of hundreds of thousands racing at 10:00 AM, fans express demand over four days; the rights owner understands the market and then deliberately allocates constrained inventory.” Then note the critical failure modes in `EXPERIMENTS.md`.
